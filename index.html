How it works

01

Scan

Players check in at the door by scanning their QR code. No sign-up sheets, no name-calling, no double entries.

02

Sit

The app assigns seats, balances tables as the field shrinks, and sends every player straight to their chair.

03

Play

A live tournament clock and player board run on the venue's TVs. Results, season standings and rewards post the moment the night ends.

Built for leagues

Check-in that takes seconds

Phones, a tablet at the door, or a dedicated scanner station. Guests and dealers are handled too.

Seating and table balancing

Automatic seat assignment, rebuys, busts and final-table consolidation without a clipboard.

The clock on the big screen

Blind levels, breaks, players remaining and chip counts on any TV, with optional announcements.

Points, standings and rewards

Season points, championship qualification and venue reward programs, tracked automatically.

A venue view

Host venues see attendance and reward performance without any player's personal details.

Your league, your brand

League colors and logo on the player screens and TVs. The platform stays behind the scenes.
